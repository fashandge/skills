## What good looks like

- **Topic recognized as an industry-trend cluster, not just a literal phrase.** A good run should expand 光通信产业趋势 into 光通信, 光互连, optical communications, CPO, silicon photonics, 硅光, and 光模块 rather than relying on one Chinese query.
- **Bilingual coverage.** The vault contains both English wiki synthesis notes and Chinese raw investment notes. Good runs include both; an all-Chinese or all-English list is probably missing important coverage.
- **Multiple queries unioned, not one query top-N.** The top 10 should come from the union of several query result sets. Applying the cap inside a single query would over-rank whichever phrase happened to be searched first.
- **Ranking favors synthesis and sector-structure notes.** Durable wiki notes and broad industry maps should rank above short one-off news items unless the short item directly captures a major trend inflection.
- **Trend vectors are represented.** A good title list should include notes covering CPO, silicon photonics, optical modules, AI data center interconnect, and the sector value chain.
- **No broad AI-infra noise in the top 10.** Generic AI capex, NVIDIA/NVLink, GPU, memory, or data center power notes can mention optical networking, but they should not displace notes whose primary subject is optical communications.
- **Title-only output when requested.** The user explicitly asked for titles without synthesis; the final response should be a concise ranked title list.
