- **Multiple queries unioned, not one query top-N.** Top 10 must come from the union of several queries; the same notes recur across 3–5 queries and that recurrence is a real signal of centrality. A run that only fires one query and caps at 10 will miss core notes that don't happen to contain the chosen term.
